# Aqua Regia

Aqua Regia is the one and only currency in Alchemize. You earn it by shipping projects and spend it in the shop.

---

## Earning Aqua Regia

For every approved hour on a project, you earn **1 Aqua Regia**, worth **$5**.

It works the same way on every theme. Endless, No Internet, Indie Gamedev and general projects all earn Aqua Regia at the same rate, so pick whichever theme you enjoy most.

Examples:

* 15 approved hours → 15 Aqua Regia ($75)
* 8 approved hours → 8 Aqua Regia ($40)
* 1 hour 30 minutes approved → 1.5 Aqua Regia ($7.50)

Only approved hours count. Reviewers can deduct hours from a submission (for example, time that doesn't meet the rules), and you are credited for the approved hours only.

---

## Your Balance

Hours are credited to the minute, so your balance is shown with up to two decimals (for example, **66.4 Aqua Regia**).

You can see your balance on your dashboard.

---

## Spending Aqua Regia

Every item in the shop is priced in Aqua Regia. There are no theme-exclusive items: anything in the shop can be bought with the Aqua Regia you earned on any theme.

New items are added regularly, so check back often!
